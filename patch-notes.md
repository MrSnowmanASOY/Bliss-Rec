# Patch Notes :greenpatchwork:
-# Version 0.1.5
## Synopsys
- Astra Nerf
- Fire Buff
- Gust Nerf
- Pulse Nerf
- Speed Buff
- Strength Nerf
- Wealth Buff
- Re-roll recipe tweak

## Shard Changes
### Astra Shard:
- Arrows now fire slightly ahead of the player to stop people from jumping into them
- Arrows no longer pierce

### Fire Shard:
- Player now leaves a trail of fire behind them for the duration of their fire resistance

### Gust Shard:
- Gust power reduced by 33.333% to stop players killing themselves while misusing the shard

### Pulse Shard:
- Point blank damage reduced 25 -> 24

### Speed Shard:
- Shard cooldown reduced 30s -> 20s

### Strength Shard:
- Duration of all effects increased by 3 seconds to compensate for charge up time
- Slowness Amp reduced 9 -> 3
- Strength Amp reduced 5 -> 3
- Resistance Amp Reduced 4 -> 3

### Wealth Shard:
- Effect added ++ Luck | Duration: 2m | Amp: 5 | Applied in a 20 block radius to up to 4 bonus players

## Tweaks
- Shard re-roll recipe tweaked

## Fixes
- Pulse shard no longer plays sound globally.
